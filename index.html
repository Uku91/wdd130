Abuja

Abuja is the capital city of Nigeria, located at the geographic center of the country within the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). It sits in the middle of Nigeria, north of the meeting point of the Niger and Benue rivers. The area features rolling hills, high plains, and isolated rock formations called inselbergs. It has a tropical climate with a warm wet season and a hot dry season.
